31. **Crash retries (`EnvConfig.retry`) and run `bcn_f1_r4_retry`**, from scratch: after a terminal failure, 75 % of the next training resets retry it, handed over at the previous corner exit (settle 0.5-0.8), and the first 3 s of the policy's own run-up are never trained on (warm-up mask in BonusNStepReplayBuffer, R2D2-style burn-in; Ibrahim's "timer before the reward", made an exclusion rather than reward 0, which would make the reward depend on an unobserved timer). Literature checked: restarting from chosen / earlier states is established (Go-Explore, Nature 2021; reverse curriculum, Florensa 2017; restart distributions, Tavakoli 2018, who warn that shifting the start distribution can bias the policy, hence 25 % uniform starts); no published "reward off after reset" method found. Plus lr decay 3M -> 6.2M to 1/100. Line IQP w3.0, recipe f1_r4_scratch as bcn_f1_r4.
